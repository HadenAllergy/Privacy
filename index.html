Haden Allergy App

Privacy Policy — Effective August 14, 2026

Summary: The Haden Allergy App stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal information. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

1. Information We Collect

Data stored on your device only:

  • Medications: Names, categories, and reminder settings you enter
  • Symptom journal entries: Dates, severity scores, and notes you record
  • App preferences: Your selected country/region and display settings
  • Your selected location: The city or coordinates you set are saved on your device so the app can load your local data the next time you open it

Location data:

  • When you grant permission, the app uses your device's GPS to fetch pollen counts and air quality data for your area
  • Your coordinates are sent directly to the pollen, air quality, and geocoding providers listed in Section 3 to retrieve local data
  • We do not have access to your location — it goes directly from your device to these providers

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The Haden Allergy App does not:

  • Require or create user accounts
  • Collect names, email addresses, or any personal identifiers
  • Use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind
  • Use advertising identifiers or ad networks
  • Use cookies
  • Send any data to our servers (we don't operate data collection servers)
  • Share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party

3. Third-Party Services

The app connects to these external services to provide pollen, air quality, location, and reference data. In every case, the request goes directly from your device to the provider — we never see, receive, or store it.

Google Pollen API — primary pollen source

  • Data sent: Latitude and longitude coordinates only
  • Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Open-Meteo — pollen fallback and air quality

  • Data sent: Latitude and longitude coordinates only
  • Their privacy policy: open-meteo.com/en/terms

Ambee, via RapidAPI — pollen fallback

  • Data sent: Latitude and longitude coordinates only, routed through the RapidAPI gateway to Ambee
  • RapidAPI's privacy policy: rapidapi.com/privacy
  • Ambee's privacy policy: getambee.com/privacy-policy

OpenStreetMap Nominatim — geocoding (turning place names into coordinates)

  • Data sent: City/location search text or coordinates
  • Their privacy policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

GitHub Pages (hadenallergy.github.io) — hosts this policy, the epinephrine device guides, and the shared emergency-card viewer

  • Data sent: Standard web requests from your browser when you open these pages — the kind every website receives
  • Like any website host, GitHub keeps standard server access logs; we never see them. Their privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement

Sharing your emergency card: when you share your card as a link, the card's contents are encoded into the part of the web address after the # symbol. Browsers never send that part to any server — not to us, not to GitHub. The card is decoded and displayed on the recipient's screen, and is never uploaded or stored anywhere.

All services are accessed over encrypted HTTPS connections. Requests may carry the app's own API keys, which identify the app to the provider — never you. The app has no user accounts or personal identifiers, and none are sent with any request.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All app data is stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults and the app's local storage
  • Data is protected by your device's built-in encryption (iOS Data Protection)
  • No data is stored on external servers
  • You can delete all app data at any time using the "Clear All Data" button in the app's My Profile tab
  • Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device

5. Children's Privacy

The Haden Allergy App does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Since the app collects no personal data from any user, it is compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

6. Medical Disclaimer

The Haden Allergy App is for informational purposes only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Pollen counts are modeled regional estimates, not clinical-grade measurements. Always consult your physician or qualified healthcare provider with questions about a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it based on information from this app.

7. Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device and we have no access to it:

  • You have full control over your data at all times
  • You can view, modify, or delete any data within the app
  • You can delete all data using the "Clear All Data" function
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data
  • There is no account to delete because no accounts exist

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version at this URL and update the effective date below. Material changes are also reflected in the app's own disclaimer and data-source notices.
